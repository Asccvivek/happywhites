Operational Bottleneck Analysis

Where Premium Dental Clinics Lose ₹10L – ₹18L Annually

Even top multispecialty dental setups leak high-margin revenue through 4 unaddressed operational friction points.

Friction Point #1

After-Hours Patient Inquiry Leakage

Over 42% of patients research dental treatments after 8:00 PM when front-desk staff is away. Unanswered calls and delayed WhatsApp replies cause patients to book consultations with competing clinics.

Estimated Loss: 12-18 high-ticket aligner & implant inquiries/month
Friction Point #2

The 25% – 30% OPD No-Show Trap

Manual calling to confirm appointments consumes 2+ receptionist hours daily, yet patients still forget. An empty operatory chair costs ₹1,500 – ₹3,500 per lost hour in sterilized downtime and doctor opportunity cost.

Estimated Loss: ₹75,000 – ₹1,20,000 in lost chair time monthly
Friction Point #3

Fragmented Clinical Records & X-Rays

Patient notes on physical registers, OPG X-rays on a local workstation, and intraoral scans on another drive. When a patient returns after 6 months, doctors waste 5–10 minutes hunting for previous treatment history.

Estimated Loss: Impaired patient trust & compromised clinical audit trail
Friction Point #4

Lab Case Delays & Stock Spoilage

TruAlign aligner batches and Zirconia crown delivery statuses tracked in chat threads. High-cost items (implant posts, bonding resins) risk expiring unnoticed or being out-of-stock when the patient is already seated in the chair.

Estimated Loss: Embarrassing patient reschedules & ₹30K/mo inventory waste
The Solution: Replace manual guesswork with an interconnected digital workflow engine.
